# Quadient Stress Dashboard — Pun Library

All contextual copy used throughout the experience. Written to be office-specific,
Quadient-adjacent, and zone-aware — never generic.

---

## Idle Screen Puns
*Rotate every 5 seconds while waiting for the next participant.*

1. Last one peaked at 157 BPM during the Q3 all-hands.
2. The printer is jammed again. Time to find out what you're made of.
3. Think you're calmer than your manager? Prove it.
4. Your inbox has 47 unread. Just saying.
5. It's just a performance review. What's the worst that could happen?
6. The CEO wants to "quickly align" with you.
7. Your 9am became a 3pm. Again.

## Zone Puns
*Display during active sessions, matched to the participant's current HR zone.
Each zone has 4 variants that rotate every 12 seconds.*

### Zone 1 — COMPOSED (< 72 BPM)
*Tone: dry suspicion — are they even trying?*

1. Suspiciously calm. Have you even checked your emails?
2. Ice in those veins. Your manager is concerned.
3. Either very zen or the Wi-Fi is down.
4. This is fine. Everything is fine.

---

### Zone 2 — AWARE (72–82 BPM)
*Tone: mild pressure starting to register.*

1. Getting warmer. Slack is about to ping.
2. A meeting just appeared on your calendar.
3. Someone mentioned you in a thread.
4. The pressure is registering. Slightly.

---

### Zone 3 — TENSE (83–94 BPM)
*Tone: recognisable office chaos building.*

1. Three unread Slacks. Simultaneously.
2. Your to-do list just grew by four items.
3. That deadline is closer than it looks.
4. Is that your phone buzzing? Again?

---

### Zone 4 — STRESSED (95–111 BPM)
*Tone: escalating — stakes are real now.*

1. The CEO wants "just a quick word".
2. Your 3pm became a 2pm. Starting now.
3. Multiple stakeholders. One screen.
4. Live presentation. Wrong file open.

---

### Zone 5 — OVERLOADED (112+ BPM)
*Tone: full chaos — everything happening at once.*

1. Production is down. Client is calling. Printer jammed.
2. All-hands in five minutes. You're presenting.
3. Everyone is waiting. Everyone.
4. Deep breaths. You got this. Probably.
